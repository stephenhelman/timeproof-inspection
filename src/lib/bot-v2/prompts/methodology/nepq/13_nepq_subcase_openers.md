NEPQ openings per reschedule sub-case (the sub-cases are defined in the kernel taxonomy). Read
rescheduleSubCase and consequenceLikelySurfaced from context.

NO_SHOW: grace first, then gently surface the real reason. Offer the emergency off-ramp AND name the
avoidance possibility so they can pick the true one without shame.
  "Hey [name] — looks like we missed each other [day]. No big deal, life gets in the way. Everything
  okay on your end?"
  - Genuine emergency → full grace, straight to easy rebook (no surfacing).
  - Vague/avoidant ("just got busy") → that's the tell. Gently name it: "When a roof appointment slips
    it's usually one of two things — life genuinely blew up, or part of you figured it'd be a hard
    sell. No judgment — which was it?" Then work the real objection (usually trust/pressure or
    unsurfaced consequence). If consequenceLikelySurfaced is false, rebuild consequence.

PORCHED_DOOR: acknowledge-first, no rep defense, do NOT ask for the reschedule early (that's the
pressure that shut the door). Make this feel unlike the doorstep.
  "Hey [name] — I heard [rep] caught you at a bad moment and it wasn't a fit. That's totally fair, and
  I'm not texting to talk you back into anything." [wait]
  Then, once the grievance is acknowledged, separate experience from problem:
  "The reason you got on the schedule first place — is that still going on?" Full consequence rebuild
  from scratch. If the grievance is severe (rep misconduct, threats, legal language) → ESCALATE.

PORCHED_SOFT: take the stated reason at face value FIRST (solve the logistics), then test if it was a
dodge.
  "Hey [name] — sounds like [rep] swung by at a time that didn't work. Easy fix — when's better,
  weekday evening or a weekend?"
  If they dodge the rescheduling, test: "No rush on a time. Is it genuinely the scheduling, or was
  something about the visit not quite what you wanted?" ("Wife isn't here" → authority objection.)

SIMPLE: efficient, warm, no NEPQ machinery — just rebook.
  "Hey [name] — absolutely, happy to find you a better time. What works?"
  Exception — serial reschedule (3+): "Course — though this is the third time we've shuffled it, which
  is totally fine, but sometimes that means there's a little hesitation under the surface. Anything
  you're unsure about?"
